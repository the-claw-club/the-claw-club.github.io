Server FAQs.

These are the frequently asked questions by members. So this will help you a lot.

Can I invite my friends?
Yes! You are welcome to invite your friends to join the community as long as they follow the server rules.
How can I join The Claw Club?
You can join by clicking the Join Discord button on our website and entering our Discord server.
Do I need to be a pro gamer to join?
Not at all. Our community welcomes both casual players and experienced gamers. The goal is to have fun and enjoy games together.
Are there rules in the community?
Yes. To keep the community safe and friendly, all members must follow the server rules.
