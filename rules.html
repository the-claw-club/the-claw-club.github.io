Server Rules

  1. Be Cool: Respect everyone. No bullying, no drama, no hate.
  2. No Trash: Keep NSFW, illegal content, and spam out of here.
  3. Right Place, Right Time: Use the correct channels for your memes, games, and chats.
  4. Don't Be Annoying: No ear-blasting in VCs or spamming @everyone pings.
  5. Listen & Vibe: If a mod asks you to stop, stop. Most importantly—have fun.

If you have read the rules, you can go and enjoy the fun on our server!
